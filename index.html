📊 Link Budget Analysis (2.4 GHz ESP-NOW)

Parameters

Tx Power (ESP32 max)+20 dBm
Tx Antenna Gain (PCB)+2 dBi
Rx Antenna Gain (PCB)+2 dBi
Frequency2.4 GHz
Rx Sensitivity (ESP32)−97 dBm

Range vs. Margin

FSPL (dB) = 40 + 20·log₁₀(dmeters)  |  RSSI = 24 − FSPL  |  Margin = RSSI − (−97)

PDR Sigmoid Model

PDR(%) = 100 / (1 + e0.2 × (−75 − RSSI))  →  50% at −75 dBm, ~99% above −50 dBm, ~5% below −90 dBm
